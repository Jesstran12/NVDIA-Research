### What changed from Edition A to Edition B, and why?

The **action did not change** — watch/defer in Edition A, do-not-initiate-at-this-price-and-watch in Edition B — but
what stands behind it did. Edition A deferred because nothing was measured: no verified price, no share count, no
forecast. Edition B defers because everything was measured and the measurement says the price pays for more than the
filings show. Three things drove the movement:

1. **Course instruction** gave the method: the FCFF/WACC architecture, the pro-forma as a labelled assumption set with
   a check block, the frozen peer policy, the one-at-a-time sensitivity and the reverse DCF. Without it Edition A's
   unknowns would have stayed a list.
2. **Own analysis** supplied the numbers and the judgments: the FY2027E build from guidance, the 25 → 4% deceleration,
   the 74.5 → 70% margin drift, the 12% cost of equity against a measured 13–14.8%, and the decision to date the bridge
   at the opening balance sheet rather than the newer 10-Q so that no cash flow is counted twice.
3. **Peer challenge** changed the shape of the downside. My partner's Apple comparison ("Apple gives up growth for
   stability; what happens to NVIDIA if AI stalls?") turned a ±5 pp sensitivity into a low case that moves growth,
   margin, opex and the discount rate together ($80) and set the −25 pp growth bound as the "AI capex stops" case.

**AI's part** was acceleration with checks, not judgment: it generated the engine from my request (checked on the ABG
known answer), built the Project 1 layer (checked on the training-case DCF and 17 tests), and proposed three things I
did not accept as proposed — the July 2026 balance sheet for the bridge (corrected: double counting), non-marketable
securities as a bridge asset (rejected: no observable price), and dropping AMD from the comps (qualified instead: kept
under the frozen policy, reported, marked not meaningful). Each disposition is in `docs/validation-and-ai-use.md`.

**What I would have to see to change the call:** two quarters of Data Center growth and margin guidance consistent
with a 35 / 22 / 17 / 14% path (the high case), or a price below $124. Both are observable by February 2027.
