## Feedback

- **Strength:** a clear economic story for the gap. Limited AI exposure explains both lower growth and lower risk, which ties the selection reason (stability) to the valuation.
- **Improvement:** quantify the story. Run a reverse DCF for the growth rate implied by $326 and add an AI-services case (e.g. a fee on a share of iOS users) to see whether it plausibly closes the gap. Since stability is the claim, also check whether the discount rate reflects Apple's lower risk.
